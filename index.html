Policy

Everything you need to know before your shoot.

Bookings & deposits

A non-refundable deposit is required to secure your date. Your booking is only confirmed once the deposit is received. The remaining balance is due on or before the shoot date, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Cancellations & rescheduling

  • Deposits are non-refundable but may be transferred to a new date once, if you reschedule at least 48 hours in advance.
  • Cancellations within 48 hours of the shoot forfeit the deposit.
  • If Shot by A needs to reschedule due to unforeseen circumstances, you'll be offered the next available date or a full refund of your deposit.

Transport & travel

All packages exclude transport costs. Travel is quoted separately based on the shoot location and included in your confirmation before you commit.

Delivery & turnaround

Edited images are delivered via a private online gallery. Standard turnaround times are agreed before your shoot. A rush-delivery option is available for an additional fee. Raw, unedited files are not included unless agreed upfront.

Image usage & rights

Shot by A retains copyright of all images. You're granted full personal usage rights to share and print your gallery. Shot by A may feature images for portfolio and social media use unless you request otherwise in writing before the shoot.

Conduct on the day

To keep every session enjoyable and safe, we ask for mutual respect on set. Shot by A reserves the right to end a session early in cases of unsafe or abusive behaviour, with no refund.
